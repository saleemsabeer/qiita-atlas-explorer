    About this map

    Each of the points is one full-text paper on the human microbiome, published 2010–2026 in journals and linked from the Qiita platform. The arrangement is the paper's published two-dimensional layout, computed from text similarity: papers that use similar language sit near each other.

    The third dimension here is presentation, not measurement. The map is a flat plane filmed by a moving camera. The optional relief mode lifts points by local paper density and says so on screen. The axes have no units.

    Every paper keeps its identity: hover for the title, click to open it on PubMed. Disease labels are nodes of the MONDO Disease Ontology, release 2026-09-01; body sites use the atlas's seven classes. Colors follow histology stains, the palette microscopists use on human tissue.

    The data embedded in this page is identical to the published atlas. The contour lines and territory labels are derived from point density for reading, nothing scientific was added or changed.
